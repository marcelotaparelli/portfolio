Context

As a developer at EVAG, I worked on automation between Google Drive and WordPress, associated with the automated generation of petition pages.

This case focuses on the automated process. No additional product is presented as part of this work.

Problem and workflow

The need addressed is generating petition pages through a workflow between Google Drive and WordPress.

At the level authorized for publication, the workflow can be described as:

Google Drive → automation → WordPress pages.

This description does not specify file formats, triggers, execution frequency, intermediate services, or authentication mechanisms.

My contribution

I designed and implemented the flow that turns Drive content into published WordPress pages, as part of systems and automation work at EVAG.

The automation reduced the manual steps in the publishing process and made page generation more direct. No published metrics quantify that gain in time saved, error reduction, or other indicators.
