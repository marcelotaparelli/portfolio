## Context

I have worked as a developer at EVAG since April 2024, on work involving systems, websites, maintenance, and solving operational problems through software.

## Operational problem

Internal support demands needed organization, traceability, and flow. The answer was a software solution dedicated to that process.

## The solution

An internal request management solution integrated with GitLab, built to support the organization, traceability, and flow of support demands. The solution has a public access URL, but that does not make its data, internal workflows, or client information public.

## My contribution

I built the solution and connected support to GitLab, giving demands a traceable flow. This account covers my technical part — it does not imply sole authorship of the product or all its decisions.

## Operational value

The value is qualitative: organized, traceable demands with flow supported by the GitLab integration. This account contains no percentages, volumes, or time, adoption, or productivity metrics — only what has been confirmed about the process.

## Evidence and limitations

The public URL identifies the solution; presenting it requires neither access to authenticated areas nor exposure of data. Internal architecture, security, business rules, and implementation details remain outside the public account until confirmed and authorized for sharing.
